# NyayaLens — Responsible AI

NyayaLens uses generative AI to help users understand legal documents. It is **not an AI lawyer**: it does not represent users, create an attorney-client relationship, guarantee legal outcomes, or replace a qualified legal professional.

## Core Principles

- **Accuracy & grounding** — answers are grounded in the uploaded document via retrieval; fabricated or ungrounded content is rejected.
- **Citation validity** — every claim carries citations that are validated against the source before being returned.
- **Honest abstention** — when there is no evidence or no model available, the system says so instead of producing plausible-sounding content.
- **Transparency** — users see what the system stated and on what evidence.
- **User control, uncertainty, accessibility and human review** are respected throughout.
