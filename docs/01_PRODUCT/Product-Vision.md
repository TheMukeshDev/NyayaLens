# NyayaLens — Product Vision

**Tagline:** Understand. Review. Act.

---

## 1. Vision

> **Make legal documents easier to understand, review, and act upon—without pretending to replace a lawyer.**

NyayaLens aims to become a trusted AI-assisted layer between complex legal documents and the people who need to understand them.

---

## 2. Mission

NyayaLens's mission is to make legal-document understanding:

* More accessible
* More transparent
* More privacy-conscious
* More actionable
* More grounded in the user's actual document

---

## 3. Product Philosophy

NyayaLens follows five principles.

### 1. Grounded Before Generative

The system should prefer evidence from the user's document before generating an answer.

### 2. Explain, Don't Overclaim

The system should explain what the document says without claiming to provide definitive legal advice.

### 3. Show the Source

Important document-grounded answers should provide supporting citations.

### 4. Privacy by Design

Legal documents should be treated as sensitive data.

### 5. Human Review Matters

The product should help users prepare for professional review rather than discourage it.

---

## 4. Product Promise

NyayaLens promises:

> **Upload a legal document and get a clearer understanding of what it contains, what deserves attention, what changed, and what questions you may want to ask next.**

## 6. Product Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Understand

Transform complex document language into understandable summaries.

### Pillar 2 — Review

Surface important clauses, obligations, dates, amounts, and areas requiring attention.

### Pillar 3 — Ask

Provide document-grounded conversational Q&A.

### Pillar 4 — Verify

Connect answers to actual document sources.

### Pillar 5 — Compare

Make document-version differences easier to identify.

### Pillar 6 — Act

Turn findings into checklists and professional discussion questions.

---

## 7. Differentiation

NyayaLens is not designed as:

```text
"Ask an AI lawyer anything."
```

Instead, it is designed as:

```text
"Understand your document with evidence."
```

The key differentiators are:

* Document-first AI
* Source-grounded answers
* Clause-level analysis
* Attention-oriented review
* Version comparison
* Action planning
* Responsible AI boundaries
* Privacy-focused architecture

---

## 8. Long-Term Vision

Future versions may support:

* Multilingual legal-document explanations
* Voice-based document Q&A
* Collaborative document review
* Professional review workflows
* Lawyer-facing workspaces
* Organization-level document management
* More document types
* Improved accessibility features

These are not required for the MVP.

---

---

## 10. Vision Statement

> **NyayaLens envisions a world where understanding a legal document does not require immediately becoming an expert in legal language.**
