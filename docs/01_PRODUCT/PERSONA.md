# NyayaLens — User Personas

---

## Persona 1 — The Employee

### Profile

**Name:** Ananya
**Age:** 24
**Role:** Software Engineer
**Experience:** Early career

### Situation

Ananya receives an employment agreement containing:

* Compensation details
* Probation conditions
* Notice period
* Confidentiality
* Intellectual property
* Termination clauses

She wants to understand the agreement before signing.

### Goals

* Understand the contract
* Find important clauses
* Know the notice period
* Understand termination conditions
* Prepare questions for HR or a lawyer

### Pain Points

* Legal terminology
* Long documents
* Fear of overlooking something
* Difficulty identifying important clauses

### NyayaLens Solution

```text
Upload agreement
→ Summary
→ Clause Explorer
→ Attention Analysis
→ Ask Questions
→ Generate Questions for Professional
```

# Persona 5 — The Legal Professional

### Profile

**Name:** Meera
**Age:** 35
**Role:** Legal Professional

### Situation

Meera wants to use AI as an efficiency tool without losing control over the source material.

### Goals

* Quickly locate clauses
* Review extracted information
* Navigate long documents
* Validate AI-generated summaries

### Requirements

* Strong citations
* Document traceability
* Clear AI limitations
* Easy source navigation

### NyayaLens Solution

The product can eventually provide professional-oriented review workflows.

This is a secondary MVP persona.

# Primary Persona

For the MVP, the primary persona is:

> **A non-lawyer reviewing a legal document who wants a fast, understandable, source-grounded explanation.**

# Common User Need

Across all personas:

```text
"I don't necessarily need an answer from a lawyer right now.
I need to understand what this document says and know what I should ask next."
```

That is the central user need NyayaLens addresses.
